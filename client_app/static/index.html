How the demo works

FreshBasket is a pretend fruit shop. Its online store (this page) is a client of StockPilot, which the shop's staff use to run the business. Everything you do here goes through StockPilot's API.

  1. Shop as a customer. Add fruit to your basket and check out. Stock shown here is live StockPilot inventory.
  2. Be the shop staff. Open StockPilot → Delivery. Your order is there. Move it to Packed, then Out for delivery (this takes the fruit off the shelf).
  3. Watch it live. Your order page here updates by itself, and the stock on the shop page drops. StockPilot's Dashboard and Inventory analytics update too.
  4. Deliver it. Mark it Delivered in StockPilot. The shop records the payment, so it appears in StockPilot → Accounts. Mark it Failed instead and the stock goes back.
  5. Restock. Receive stock on StockPilot → Inventory and it shows up here instantly.
